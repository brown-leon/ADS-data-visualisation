# Executive Sales & Operations Dashboard

**Audience:** Senior management and operations leadership  
**Purpose:** Provide a high-level view of sales performance and identify the operational drivers behind revenue trends.

## What this dashboard answers

- Is overall sales performance improving over time?
- Are revenue changes driven by pricing or transaction volume?
- Where and when is operational demand concentrated?

## Design focus

- Clear visual hierarchy separating executive summary from operational detail
- Trend-focused visuals to support performance monitoring
- Minimal colour and reduced clutter to prioritise decision-making

## Notes

This dashboard was developed as part of a Data Visualization course project.  
Detailed design rationale and methodology are documented separately.
